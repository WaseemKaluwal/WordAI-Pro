# ⚡ WordAI Pro — Microsoft Word AI Add-in

AI writing assistant, document automation suite, and academic research copilot that lives directly inside Microsoft Word (Word Online & Word Desktop). No tab switching, zero backend needed (BYOK).

🔗 **Live Add-in:** https://waseemkaluwal.github.io/wordai-pro/taskpane.html

---

## 🚀 Complete 30-Feature Wishlist & Capabilities Matrix

WordAI Pro is fully equipped with all 30 features requested by students, academic researchers, and professional writers:

### 🌟 1. High Priority (Most Requested by Students/Writers)
1. **🔍 Plagiarism & Originality Highlighter:** Scans text to compute an originality score percentage, highlights unoriginal or clichéd phrases, explains stylistic weaknesses, and provides fresh 1-click academic rewrites.
2. **📚 AI Citation Finder:** Enter any claim or topic $\rightarrow$ AI suggests 3–5 real seminal papers and books with direct, clickable search links to **Google Scholar**, **PubMed**, and **JSTOR**.
3. **📋 Smart Templates Library:** 1-click structural generation directly into your document for:
   - *Research Proposal* (Problem statement, hypotheses, methodology, work plan)
   - *Thesis Chapter* (Chapter 3 methodology blueprint)
   - *Lab Report* (Objectives, apparatus, data tables, percent error, discussion)
   - *Executive Business Plan* (Value prop, TAM, competitor matrix, financials)
   - *Press Release* (Standard PR distribution format with boilerplate & contact)
4. **🎯 Word Count Target Tracker:** Set word count goals (e.g. 5,000 words); live progress bar tracks real-time count, % completed, and remaining words needed.
5. **📊 Reading Level Analyzer:** Flesch Reading Ease score, Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level, reading time estimate, syllable count, and target audience suitability.

### 🎓 2. Research Copilot Upgrades
6. **🔬 Research Gap Finder:** Paste abstracts or topic notes $\rightarrow$ AI identifies unexamined populations, methodological limitations, and formulates 3 high-impact research questions to fill the void.
7. **💡 Thesis Statement Generator:** Enter your topic and central argument $\rightarrow$ AI crafts 5 distinct, debate-ready thesis statements (Analytical, Argumentative, Cause-and-Effect, Comparative, Policy).
8. **⚖️ Counter-Argument Builder:** Formulates the 3 strongest opposing arguments a referee or debater would raise, complete with opposition evidence and strategic rebuttal blueprints.
9. **🎤 Interview Questions Generator:** Qualitative research protocol generator producing rapport-building warm-ups, core phenomenological inquiries, follow-up probes, and concluding reflections.
10. **📋 Survey / Questionnaire Builder:** Generates ready-to-deploy Likert-scale statements (1–5) and demographic/open-ended questions free of acquiescence and double-barreled biases.
- **🔍 Live Academic Paper Search (Semantic Scholar):** Search 200M+ real academic papers with live DOIs, abstracts, and 1-click in-text and bibliographic insertion.
- **🎓 1-Click APA 7th & MLA 9th Formatting:** Standardizes margins (1"), Times New Roman 12pt, and double-spacing across the entire document.
- **🔬 Reviewer #2 Peer Review Mode:** Demanding referee critique evaluating novelty, methodology rigor, and official verdict (Accept/Minor/Major/Reject).

12. **🌉 Transition Sentence Generator:** Analyzes two consecutive paragraphs and crafts 3 smooth conceptual bridges and transition sentences.
13. **🧱 Paragraph Expander with Evidence Slots:** Deepens analytical density and explicitly places bracketed `[ADD EVIDENCE HERE: metric/source needed]` slots for empirical rigor.

- **🛡️ Contract & Legal Risk Scanner:** Detects unlimited liability, indemnification traps, auto-renewals, and ambiguous clauses with recommended counter-redlines.

## 💻 Installation (Word Online — Free)

1. Open **[office.com](https://www.office.com)** and sign in with a free Microsoft account $\rightarrow$ open any document.
2. Go to **Insert** $\rightarrow$ **Add-ins** $\rightarrow$ **My Add-ins** $\rightarrow$ **Upload My Add-in**.
3. Upload `manifest.xml`.
4. Click **⚙️ Settings** in the task pane $\rightarrow$ choose your provider $\rightarrow$ paste your API key $\rightarrow$ click **🔄 Fetch Models** $\rightarrow$ Save.

---

## 💻 Installation (Word Desktop)

```bash
npm install
npx office-addin-dev-certs install
npm start
```

This starts the local dev server and sideloads the add-in into Microsoft Word automatically.
